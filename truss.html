Truss and gable studs

The 2×4 truss goes against the front face of the gable board, with its whole bottom cut level with the gable board’s bottom and the upper plate’s top. The upper piece is 54 inches and the lower, steeper piece is 37¾ inches, measured between their longest points. The highest peak is 4 feet above the top of the upper plate.

A box that follows the window

For a real gable window, the studs move to its two sides and horizontal 2×4s form the top and bottom. All four 3½-inch faces point outward. The opening determines the measurements; there is no single window size for every barn.

Gable backing

When this gable has no window or fake window, horizontal 2×4 gable backing fits between the gable studs. Its 3½-inch face points outward in line with the studs. Measure 11 inches from the top of the upper plate to the bottom of the backing.
